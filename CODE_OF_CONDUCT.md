# Code of Conduct

This project follows the Netherlands eScience Center commitment to an open, inclusive, and respectful research software community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and ask when something is unclear.
- Make space for different expertise levels and backgrounds.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, documentation, and process.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, personal attacks, or sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, contact the project maintainers through the repository or the Netherlands eScience Center contact channels.
